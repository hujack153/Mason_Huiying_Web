Why Huiying

More than materials

Systems, not single products

Waterproofing usually fails between layers rather than within them. We specify the primer, membrane and topcoat as one system so incompatible products are never mixed.

Specification driven by site conditions

Substrate, location, climate and programme all affect the choice. Rather than promoting one product, we look at the site first and recommend accordingly.

Support on site

Substrate preparation, coverage rates, temperature, humidity and recoat intervals all determine the outcome. We can provide a technical briefing and sample panel before work starts.
