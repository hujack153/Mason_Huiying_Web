About Huiying

What we offer is judgement: the right material, specified as a complete system, with someone who will solve problems on site.

Huiying Int'l Business Co. is based in Xinzhuang District, New Taipei City, supplying construction waterproofing materials, exterior decorative coatings and epoxy flooring systems, together with the technical service to specify and install them.

Beyond supply, we assist with material selection, sample panels and pre-start technical briefings. Waterproofing rarely fails because of one product; it fails in the interaction between layers and in the detail of installation.
