Coating system

Stone-effect wall coating

A water-based multi-colour coating closely reproducing natural granite, formed in a single spray pass — an environmentally responsible alternative to stone cladding.

Five components, none optional, each with a distinct function and coverage rate.

01

Penetrating Sealer Primer

 A high-polymer acrylic copolymer resin emulsion classed as a specialist waterproofing material.

A high-polymer acrylic copolymer resin emulsion classed as a specialist waterproofing material. Once cured over the levelling layer it forms a dense film that slows moisture loss and maintains good curing conditions. Suitable over natural stone, aggregate, limestone, cementitious surfaces, concrete, render and firebrick.

Characteristics
  • Fast drying with high penetration
  • Bonds in both directions; alkali and water resistant
  • Water-based, safe and non-toxic
  • Particularly suited to under-cured levelling layers

A single-component coating of water-based polymer and selected pigment, ready to use from the pail. Good workability, cures directly after roller application, and forms an elastic, high-strength, durable film with a high sheen.

Characteristics
  • Improves opacity
  • High penetration and sealing capability
  • Strong adhesion to cementitious surfaces
  • Suits alkali sealing on new and existing high-end substrates

Stone-Effect Coating

 Produced using an advanced granulation process, a single product contains five or more distinct colour particles.

Produced using an advanced granulation process, a single product contains five or more distinct colour particles. Sprayed in one pass, the particles merge into a film reproducing the appearance of natural granite, with genuine relief, a coarse tactile finish and rich colour.

Characteristics
  • Over 90% visual match to natural granite
  • Tightly controlled particle size and colour consistency
  • Reproduces the natural veining and texture of high-grade stone
  • Dense, hard film with water and alkali resistance

A water-based acrylic resin synthesised with silane and nano technology, forming a dense, transparent and tough film. Durable against weathering, water and staining, easy to wash down, and gives lasting protection to the coating below.

  • Improves stain and weather resistance of the coloured layer

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does stone-effect coating compare with real granite?
Visually it reaches over 90% match. The main differences are weight and cost: a coating needs no cladding support structure, which substantially reduces structural load and installation cost, and avoids the environmental impact of quarrying.
What layers make up the complete system?
Seven layers: substrate, levelling, penetrating sealer primer, pigmented base coat, stone-effect coating, clear topcoat and joint coating. The joint coating divides the elevation and is key to the realism of the finish.
How long does the full sequence take?
It is governed by recoat intervals: 8 hours after levelling, 4 hours after primer, 8 hours after base coat, and 24 hours after the stone-effect coating. In normal weather a single elevation takes four to six working days.
Can colours be specified?
Yes. We recommend producing a site sample panel for client and supervisor approval before proceeding, which then serves as the reference for handover inspection.
